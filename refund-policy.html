Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy applies to bookings for home sleep apnea testing services.

Arisen Venture provides home sleep apnea testing services. Requests for cancellation, rescheduling or refund will be assessed individually based on the circumstances of the booking and the services already provided.

A request, complaint or allegation does not automatically entitle a customer to a refund. We may reasonably request supporting information and review relevant booking, payment, communication and service records before determining whether a refund or other remedy is appropriate.

Where the booked service has been properly provided as agreed, payments are generally non-refundable for change of mind, dissatisfaction unrelated to a failure in the service, or circumstances outside our reasonable control, except where otherwise required by applicable Malaysian law.

If a service is shown not to comply with an applicable statutory consumer guarantee, we will provide the remedy required under applicable Malaysian law.

Refunds that are approved will ordinarily be made using the original payment method, where practicable. Processing times may vary depending on the bank or payment provider.

Any suspected fraudulent, misleading or unsupported refund claim may be declined, subject always to the customer's rights under applicable Malaysian law.

For cancellation, rescheduling or refund enquiries, please contact us at 012-651 3091.

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies any consumer right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded under Malaysian law.
