Common signs

Could sleep apnea be affecting you?

Symptoms can appear while you sleep or affect how you feel during the day. A home sleep apnea test may be suitable for some adults with suspected obstructive sleep apnea.

😴

Loud snoring

Persistent or disruptive snoring that may be noticed by a partner or family member.

💨

Gasping or choking

Waking suddenly or being told that you gasp, choke or appear to stop breathing during sleep.

☀️

Daytime tiredness

Feeling sleepy or unrefreshed despite spending enough time in bed.

🧠

Brain fog

Poor concentration, reduced alertness or difficulty staying focused during the day.

🌅

Morning symptoms

Waking with a dry mouth, headache or feeling that sleep was not restorative.

🌙

Restless sleep

Repeated waking, tossing and turning, or sleep that feels fragmented throughout the night.
