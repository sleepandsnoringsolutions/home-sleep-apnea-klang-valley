Could sleep apnea be affecting your sleep?

Obstructive sleep apnea can be associated with repeated breathing interruptions during sleep. A home sleep apnea test may be one way to assess suspected sleep apnea in suitable adults.

Loud snoring

Persistent or disruptive snoring, especially when noticed by a partner or family member.

Breathing pauses

Witnessed pauses in breathing, choking or gasping during sleep.

Daytime tiredness

Feeling excessively sleepy, unrefreshed or unable to concentrate during the day.
